Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

MeLift is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what information MeLift collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Workout data: the exercises, workouts, splits, sets, reps, weights, and notes you enter.

Your profile photo, if you add one, is stored only on your device and is never uploaded.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the app: to sign you in, store your workouts, and show your progress. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Where it's stored

Account and workout data are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, and are sent over encrypted connections. Each account can only access its own data.

Sharing

We don't share your information with anyone, except Supabase as described above or when required by law. Workout files you export and share are sent only to the people you choose.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Account Management → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all your workout data.

Children

MeLift isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
